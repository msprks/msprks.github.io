Pleated Fingers Parted is a solo exhibition of recent paintings by Vancouver-based artist M.E. Sparks.

This new body of work emerges from a desire to disassemble familiarized narratives within painting history. Focusing on historical representations of the female body, femininity and youth, Sparks explores the complex tensions of working from and within dominant patriarchal perspectives. The paintings reconfigure bodily forms and spaces, seeking to offer another side of the story.

For this exhibition, Sparks investigates the material autonomy of paint and canvas to emphasize the malleability, flaccidity and contradictory flatness of the painted image. Through layers, slits, folds and bends, the figurative shapes are deconstructed, or perhaps released, as if lying in wait for new postures.
